The day, meal by meal

Built from your kitchen — roti, sabji, dal, dahi. Katori = standard 150 ml bowl.

House rules

  • Paratha protocol Weekend only, max 1, made with 1 tsp oil — and skip one phulka that day. Parathas aren’t banned, they’re budgeted (~250–300 kcal each).
  • Oil budget: 3 tsp/day for all cooking, measured with a spoon, never poured. This one habit saves 200–300 kcal daily.
  • Chai: max 2, zero sugar. Sugar in tea is the stealthiest calorie source in an Indian day.
  • No liquid calories. Juice, cold drinks, sweet lassi — chew your calories, drink water or chaas.
  • Protein first on the plate — dal/paneer/curd before roti. You’ll naturally eat one roti less.
  • Dinner ≥3h before bed, sleep 7–8h. Short sleep raises hunger hormones and parks fat at the belly.
  • Sweet craving? 2 squares dark chocolate or 1 date. Budgeted, not banned.
  • One free meal a week — a meal, not a day. Enjoy it fully, return to plan the very next meal.

The truth about belly fat

You cannot spot-reduce it — no number of crunches burns fat off the stomach specifically. The deficit decides how much fat you lose; the belly is usually the last place it leaves for men. Lifting decides what’s underneath when it does. Patience + consistency beats intensity.

Why you couldn’t finish sets — and the fix

It wasn’t weakness, it was starting too heavy without a movement map. Weeks 1–2: machines and light dumbbells, watch the demo before the first set, stop with 2 reps in the tank. Film yourself from the side once a week and compare with the demo — it’s the fastest form feedback there is.

When to add weight

When you hit the top of the rep range on all sets with clean form, add 2.5 kg (upper body) or 5 kg (legs/machines) next session. The app shows a green prompt when you’ve earned it.

Desk-job counterweights

Stand and walk 3 min every hour. A 10-minute walk after lunch blunts the glucose spike and quietly banks 1,000+ steps. Park the water bottle far from the desk.

Plateau protocol

Scale frozen for 2 weeks? First check honesty — untracked bites, poured oil, weekend drift. Still stuck: either −100 kcal or +2,000 steps. Never both at once.
